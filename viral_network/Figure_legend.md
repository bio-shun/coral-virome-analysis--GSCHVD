# Figure legend

Fig. X | Viral family association networks across coral health states. a,b, Healthy/normal (exposed) (blue; n = 20) and SCTLD (red; n = 69) DNA viromes from Orbicella faveolata coral tissue at five Florida locations within PRJNA925892. Both panels show the 11-family union of stable-edge endpoints at identical coordinates. Healthy contains 17 stable associations and SCTLD contains 8; grey nodes have no stable edge in that state. Edge width denotes sign-specific retention across 500 site-balanced bootstrap resamples (20 samples per group per resample); stable associations had retention >= 50% after satisfying |rho| >= 0.7 and BH-adjusted P <= 0.05 in each resample. Gold fill marks Microviridae, the only named network family meeting the separate |LDA score| >= 2.5 display criterion (SCTLD-enriched; LDA = 2.98; raw Kruskal-Wallis P = 0.0257; within-stratum BH-adjusted P = 0.659). LDA was not used to construct or filter the network, and this annotation is exploratory. c, Absolute bootstrap retention for the 17-edge union and descriptive change in retention from Healthy to SCTLD, expressed in percentage points. Dots mark cells with retention >= 50% and are not P-value markers; no edge-wise differential test was performed. d-f, Bootstrap distributions of retained associations, connected families and connected components calculated on the prespecified full 18-family candidate set. Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range; violins and points show 500 resamples per group. Empirical two-sided P values were calculated from 999 health-label permutations within location on the full reference networks, with BH correction across eight topology metrics. The a,b union display is descriptive and does not redefine the inferential network universe. Figure source data are provided in the accompanying package.
